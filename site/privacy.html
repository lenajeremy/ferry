Ferry privacy policy

Last updated 17 October 2026

Ferry sends photos, videos and files between your own phones and computers over the network you're on. It's built so that nothing about you or your files goes anywhere else.

What Ferry collects

Nothing. Ferry has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no servers of its own. We never receive your files, your devices' names, your location or anything else.

Where your files go

Straight from one of your devices to another, across your local network. Ferry's apps encrypt everything they send. A file you send lands in the Ferry folder on your computer (or in your phone's photo library, when you choose Save) and nowhere else.

What stays on your devices

  • On your computer: the Ferry folder you share, and Ferry's own data: its pairing code, its certificate, the list of phones and computers you've paired (each with its own key) and thumbnails of your files.
  • On your phone: the computers you've paired with and this phone's key for each, in the phone's secure storage.

You can remove a paired device from your computer's Ferry page at any time, and forget a computer from the app. Uninstalling Ferry removes its data from that device.

Permissions the apps ask for

  • Camera (iPhone and Android): only to scan the code on your computer's screen. Nothing is recorded or kept.
  • Local network: to find your computers on the Wi-Fi and send files to them.
  • Photos (adding only): to save the photos and videos you choose to your library. Ferry can't read your library; you pick what to send.

Children

Ferry doesn't collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be here with a new date. Questions: open an issue on GitHub.
